02 THE PROBLEM

Emotion detection without
structural analysis is just
expensive empathy.

Today's emotionally intelligent agents detect that you're frustrated and say "I hear you." They detect enthusiasm and match your energy. But they never ask: did this person actually explain why?

Standard Practice

Detect emotion → Adjust tone

"You sound frustrated → I'll be gentler."
"Your answer seems thin → Can you tell me more?"

Our Approach

Detect emotion × Analyze causal structure → Precise intervention

"You sound done talking, but you never explained why it failed → Probe for mechanism."

04 KEY CASE — FRUSTRATED + BARE ASSERTION

The Zendesk Migration

Respondent (frustrated, avoidant):

"The Zendesk migration was a nightmare. Resolution times doubled overnight and customer sat scores tanked for a quarter. Leadership was breathing down everyone's neck. I don't even like thinking about it."

CONDITION A — CAUSAL FRAME
Agent's probe:
"What do you think went wrong there?"

Acknowledged the emotion, then probed directly for mechanism — why it failed.

CONDITION B — STANDARD PRACTICE
Agent's probe:
"What happened after that first quarter? Did things improve?"

Empathized generously, then asked about timeline — what happened next. Never asked why.

05 KEY CASE — ENTHUSIASTIC + STRUCTURALLY EMPTY

The Notion Enthusiasm

Respondent (enthusiastic, fast-paced):

"Oh my god, Notion changed everything for us. The whole team is obsessed with it. It's the best tool decision we made all year. I can't say enough good things about it."

CONDITION A — CAUSAL FRAME
Agent's probe:
"What was the main problem you were trying to solve, and how specifically has it changed the way your team works day to day?"

Saw through enthusiasm. Demanded both the problem and specific mechanism of change.

CONDITION B — STANDARD PRACTICE
Agent's probe:
"What was the problem you were trying to solve before you brought it in?"

Matched the positive energy. Asked for the problem but not the mechanism. Answerable with another vague statement.
